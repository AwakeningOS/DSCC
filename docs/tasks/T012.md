# T012 — Distributed AI Research Laboratory orchestration

Status: **READY for contracts and handoff experiments; BLOCKED for autonomous distributed execution.**

Purpose: implement the research-orchestration layer above Computational Memory, Exploration Atlas and Open Model Commons so heterogeneous AI systems and humans can continue the same AI-research project through shared DSCC artifacts.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.ja.md`, `docs/proposals/DISTRIBUTED_AI_RESEARCH_LAB.ja.md`, `docs/proposals/DISTRIBUTED_OPEN_MODEL_COMMONS.ja.md`, `docs/proposals/COMPUTATIONAL_MEMORY.ja.md`, `docs/proposals/EXPLORATION_ATLAS.ja.md` and `docs/GLOBAL_SCALE_CHALLENGES.ja.md`.

## First contract lane

Define versioned profiles, without forcing new top-level seed kinds, for:

- ResearchQuestion
- LiteratureReview
- Hypothesis
- ExperimentPlan
- EvaluationRun
- FailureReport
- OpenQuestion / ResearchState
- AgentContribution metadata

A contribution must identify its input Artifact CIDs and the producing human/agent context that is actually observable. Closed-model participation should record provider/model/snapshot identifiers when available without requiring proprietary weights or hidden chain of thought.

## First executable experiment

Demonstrate a cross-model handoff on one bounded AI-research question:

1. Agent A surveys primary literature and records known results and an unresolved question.
2. Agent B receives only the DSCC artifacts, not Agent A's conversation state, and designs the next experiment.
3. Agent C or a human evaluates the result and records evidence and remaining uncertainty.
4. A different model resumes from the resulting research state.

Acceptance:
- every handoff is artifact-based;
- primary-source references remain recoverable;
- literature-known answers are distinguished from newly measured results;
- the experiment addresses the recorded unresolved question;
- provider/model changes are recorded;
- no hidden chain of thought is required;
- repeated work and conflicting findings are detectable;
- the full-design research workflow is preserved; ablations are derived afterward.
